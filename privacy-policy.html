Privacy policy

Krown Frontz (“we”, “us”) is based in Manchester, UK. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights.

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

Krown Frontz is a custom grillz and dental jewellery maker operating from Manchester, United Kingdom. For privacy enquiries, contact krownfrontz@gmail.com.

What we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number (if provided), and delivery address.
  • Order information — products, materials, tooth selection, impression method, and order notes.
  • Payment data — payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Booking data — if you book an in-person appointment via Calendly, Calendly collects the details you provide in their widget.
  • Marketing sign-up — email address if you subscribe on the homepage.
  • Technical data — basic browser/device information and site preferences stored locally (for example, display currency) in your browser’s local storage.

How we use your data

We use personal data to:

  • Process and fulfil orders (impression kits, custom grillz, shipping).
  • Communicate about your order, appointment, or enquiry.
  • Take payment and issue refunds where applicable.
  • Send marketing emails if you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • Improve the website and prevent fraud or abuse.

We process order and fulfilment data on the basis of contract (to deliver what you bought). Marketing emails are sent only with your consent.

Who we share data with

We share data only where needed to run the business:

  • Stripe — payment processing (Stripe privacy policy).
  • Calendly — appointment booking (Calendly privacy policy).
  • Netlify — website hosting and serverless checkout functions.
  • Carriers — name and address for delivery (Royal Mail and partners).

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

How long we keep data

  • Order records — kept for up to 7 years for accounting, tax, and dispute resolution.
  • Marketing emails — until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your address.
  • Enquiry emails — kept as long as needed to resolve your request, then deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Ask us to delete your data (where we are not required to keep it).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw marketing consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

To exercise any of these rights, email krownfrontz@gmail.com.

Cookies & local storage

The site uses browser local storage to remember your cart and currency preference. We do not run third-party advertising trackers. Stripe and Calendly may set their own cookies when you pay or book an appointment through their services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
