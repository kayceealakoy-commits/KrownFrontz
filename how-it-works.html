Questions

Do I need an appointment if I order by post?

No. The kit is the appointment. You only book if you want the impression taken in person.

How much does the impression kit cost?

UK customers pay £20 all-in (materials, outbound delivery, and a prepaid UK return form). International customers pay £10 for kit materials plus regional outbound kit shipping — see our shipping policy. Return postage is not prepaid — international clients find their own way to return the kit to us, using the return address found on the postage. Finished grillz shipping is charged at checkout for everyone.

When do I pay in full for a studio appointment?

You pay a £10 deposit when you book. After your impression is taken in studio, the remaining balance is due in full before we begin making your grillz.

Where is the studio?

Manchester, UK. The exact address is sent in your booking confirmation email.

What if I mess up the impressions at home?

We will send you a new kit at an additional cost.

How long does a piece take?

Around 2–4 weeks after we have a good impression.
