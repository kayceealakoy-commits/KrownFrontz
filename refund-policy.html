Refund policy

Every Krown Frontz piece is made to order from your impressions. This policy explains when refunds, replacements, or cancellations are available.

Last updated: August 2026

Custom-made goods

Grillz and other dental jewellery are bespoke items made specifically for you from the teeth, style, and material you select at checkout. Under UK consumer law, the usual 14-day right to cancel distance contracts does not apply once production has begun on goods made to your specification.

By placing an order and submitting impressions (postal or in person), you confirm the design details and authorise us to begin work. Production cannot begin unless full payment has been made.

Cancellations before production

  • If you need to cancel before we have started making your piece, contact us as soon as possible at krownfrontz@gmail.com.
  • If your impression kit has not yet been dispatched, we will refund the full order amount minus any non-recoverable payment processing fees, where applicable.
  • If we have already dispatched an impression kit, the kit fee may be non-refundable once materials have been sent. Outbound shipping charges are not refunded if the kit has already been posted.
  • In-person appointment deposits are non-refundable if you miss your slot without reasonable notice, or if we have held the appointment time for you and you do not attend.

After production has started

Once we have begun manufacturing your grillz, orders cannot be cancelled or refunded for change of mind, incorrect tooth selection chosen by you, or dissatisfaction with a design you approved at checkout.

This does not affect your statutory rights if the item is faulty or not as described (see below).

Faulty or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or materially different from what you ordered, contact us within 14 days of delivery with:

  • Your order reference or checkout email
  • Clear photos of the issue
  • A brief description of the problem

Where a fault is confirmed, we will, at our discretion and in line with your consumer rights:

  • Repair or remake the piece at no extra cost, or
  • Offer a full or partial refund if a repair or remake is not possible.

Fit issues caused by poor-quality or incomplete impressions are not considered a manufacturing fault. We may charge for a replacement impression kit and remanufacture if new impressions are required.

Lost or damaged in transit

If a parcel is lost or arrives damaged, email us within 5 business days of the expected delivery window (see our shipping policy). We will work with the carrier to investigate and arrange a replacement shipment or refund where appropriate.

How to request a refund

  1. Email krownfrontz@gmail.com with your name, order details, and reason for the request.
  2. We will respond within 3 business days.
  3. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method via Stripe. Banks may take 5–10 business days to show the credit.
