Delivery

Shipping policy

We dispatch impression kits and finished grillz worldwide from Manchester, UK. One regional rate applies per parcel — kit outbound and finished grillz use the same price for your area.

Last updated: August 2026

How shipping is charged

Postal orders are split into clear charges so you know the full cost before you pay.

  • UK postal: Add the £20 UK impression kit to your cart (all-in — materials, outbound delivery, and prepaid return form) + finished grillz shipping at checkout.
  • International postal: Add the £10 international impression kit to your cart (materials only) + regional kit shipping at checkout for the outbound parcel (up to 500g). When your grillz are ready, finished grillz shipping is charged separately at the lower 250g rate for the same region. If both apply, checkout shows two lines. Return postage is not prepaid — international clients find their own way to return the kit to us, using the return address found on the postage. Each international kit includes: 2 dental trays, 2 blue dental putty, 2 white dental putty, and 1 silicone dispenser (enough for 2 attempts).
  • In person: collect free in Manchester, or pay finished grillz shipping only if you choose post.
  • Accessories: polishing cloth and grillz case ship at the finished / outbound rate for your region. Gift cards are digital — no shipping charge; after payment we email you a code to pass on. Grillz repolishing (£15): choose post (outbound shipping + we email the return address after payment) or drop off in Manchester via DM (no shipping).

At a glance

  • Orders are processed within 1–2 business days (Monday–Friday; orders placed before 2pm GMT dispatch same or next working day).
  • We ship to most countries worldwide. Sanctioned or restricted territories are excluded.
  • All delivery times are business days and exclude UK bank holidays and weekends.
  • Finished grillz shipping is always charged at checkout — not as a surprise later.

UK impression kit — flat fee

£20 covers kit materials, outbound delivery to your UK address, and a prepaid return postage form back to our Manchester address. Applies to United Kingdom customers only.

Each kit includes enough materials for 2 attempts:

  • 2 dental trays
  • 2 blue dental putty
  • 2 white dental putty
  • 1 silicone dispenser
  • Return postage form (UK only)

Regional shipping rates

Finished grillz shipping (up to 250g) is charged when your piece is ready — usually at a lower rate. UK customers pay £6.99 for finished grillz post; the UK impression kit is £20 all-in (materials, outbound delivery, and prepaid return). In-person customers can collect in person or choose post at these rates.

Customs & import duties

  • International orders are shipped DDU (Delivery Duty Unpaid).
  • The recipient is responsible for customs clearance, VAT/GST, and any import fees charged by their country.
  • Delays at customs are outside our control and are not included in the estimated delivery windows above.

If you are unsure what duties may apply in your country, check with your local customs authority before ordering.

Tracking & support

  • A tracking number is sent by email once your parcel has been dispatched.
  • Allow the full estimated window plus 5 business days before reporting a delay.
  • For lost or damaged parcels, contact us at krownfrontz@gmail.com with your order details.
